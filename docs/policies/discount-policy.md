# Discount policy

Volume discounts apply to invoices with more than 10 line items: 5% for 11 to 20 items and 10% for 21 or more. Discounts are applied at invoice level, never per track.

Promotional discounts require sign-off from the regional sales manager and must be recorded with a promotion code on the invoice.

Discounts cannot be combined. When two discounts could apply, the larger one is used.
