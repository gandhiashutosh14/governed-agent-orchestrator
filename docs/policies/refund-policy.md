# Refund policy

Digital tracks are refundable within 14 days of purchase if they have not been downloaded more than once. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.

Refunds above 50.00 require approval from a support team lead. Refunds above 200.00 require approval from the finance controller.

Chargebacks are handled by finance; support agents must not issue a refund for an invoice with an open chargeback.
